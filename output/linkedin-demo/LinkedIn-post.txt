I've been building ParkinSense PK to explore a practical question: can recordings of everyday movement and speech be made easier to review?

This short demo shows real-recording outputs for walking, finger tapping and voice, including 3D landmark replays, signal features and a report summary. The application also includes sit-to-stand, reach/grasp and daily-activity sections.

The latest archived software suite passed 463 tests. I also reran the three media examples used for this film. Those checks tell me the software runs; they do not establish clinical accuracy.

One finding worth sharing: video quality checks can accept the wrong exercise. Task confirmation still needs human review. Independent measurement validation, patient usability and clinic evaluation are the next steps.

This is a research prototype, not a diagnostic tool. The film is an edited visualization of recorded analysis outputs, not a live screen recording.

I'd welcome feedback from neurologists, physiotherapists and researchers on what would make these measurements useful in practice.
